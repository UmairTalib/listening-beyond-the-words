# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are recorded in this file.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Changes to method settings are listed under **Changed**, with their date and the reason.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Engineering conventions in `docs/engineering_conventions.md`, enforced by Ruff, mypy, pytest, pre-commit hooks and a CI workflow.
- Documentation index with a glossary in `docs/README.md`.
- Pilot protocol in `docs/pilot_protocol.md`: the design, the five feasibility checks, the go / no-go rule and the settings change log.
- Locked Python 3.12 environment managed with uv: the CPU build of PyTorch for the Mac and CI, the CUDA 12.6 build with V100 kernels for OMNI.
- Settings module that reads every data path from `LBW_WORKSPACE`.
- Slurm helpers: a shared job setup and a submit script that keeps logs in the workspace.
- Script that sets up the GPU environment on OMNI and checks the PyTorch build.
- Download script that fetches E-DAIC straight into the OMNI workspace, checks file sizes and records SHA-256 checksums.
- Unpack job for the audio and transcripts of the 189 pilot sessions (300–492).
- Voxtral Mini 3B pinned to a Hugging Face commit in `configs/pilot/models.yaml`, a script that fetches pinned models on an OMNI login node, and a GPU smoke test job: the model loads in fp16, "Yes" and "No" are single tokens, and synthetic clips get finite scores.
- The 189 pilot session IDs with a guard that refuses every other session, an E-DAIC transcript reader, and a transcript check that prints aggregate numbers only: lines, words, coverage, timing, confidence, fillers, tags and known interviewer phrases.
- Interviewer filter: a pinned speaker-verification model (WavLM x-vectors) scores short speech windows inside the participant's lines against the interviewer's voice, with a GPU job that prints aggregate checks and prepares a blind listening set on OMNI.
- Labels of the pilot: PHQ-8 total of 10 or more from the official split files, checked against the item scores, keeping only the 189 pilot sessions; pinned with its SHA-256 in `configs/pilot/data.yaml`.
- Line selection of the pilot: the interviewer filter applied to every transcript line, with a check that the dropped share does not differ between PHQ-8 classes or genders.
- Pilot protocol section "Speech used": the transcripts, the labels and the interviewer filter, fixed before any training.
- Word times: lines with broken timestamps are dropped, and the words of every kept line are placed on the audio by forced alignment with a pinned wav2vec2 model (GPU job).
- Segments of exactly 40 consecutive participant words, with lines joined by 300 ms gaps.
- Partitions: 2 rounds of 5 folds (seeds 101 and 102), split by participant and stratified by label and gender.
- Cut of the original clip (`x0`): each part of a segment keeps up to 0.1 s of audio around its words, never more than halfway to the nearest other kept word or past the recording, fades in and out over 10 ms and is joined to the next part by 300 ms of silence. The margin and the fade are set in `configs/pilot/data.yaml`.
- Listening check: a script on OMNI cuts 20 random segments as original clips at a common loudness, numbered in random order, with a key to open only after listening.
- Chunk check of the interviewer filter: a GPU job scores every short stretch of speech inside the listened clips on its own, with a control on known interviewer lines.
- Interviewer detector, first part: spectral fingerprints of short sound events, the search for each event's best match in every other session, and a GPU job that checks the premise (gate P1) and builds the template libraries of the interviewer's recurring clips (gate P2).
- Interviewer detector, second part: a GPU scan of every session with every template (normalised cross-correlation, on all cells and on the quiet frames), pitch decoys that set the threshold, zones, and a job that checks gates D1-D4 with planted copies and reports what the zones would cost by class and gender.
- DAIC-WOZ transcripts: a script on OMNI takes each session's human transcript, with the interviewer's lines and their times, out of the downloaded official archive. It checks each transcript against the archive's CRC-32, counts the lines per speaker, compares the archive's recording with the E-DAIC one, records a failing session and goes on, and prints aggregate numbers and session IDs, never transcript text.
- DAIC-WOZ download script: fetches the official DAIC-WOZ files straight into the OMNI workspace, apart from E-DAIC, with resumable parallel downloads, size checks against the server, SHA-256 checksums and read-only files. It refuses any session outside the 189 pilot sessions.
- Cut step: the cut rule of the pilot protocol is applied to every aligned word. The DAIC-WOZ turns are read without their text, and blank rows and sync markers are not turns. Each word is kept or dropped, with every condition it fails and one cause. Kept words get part keys, so that a part ends at a new line, a dropped word, an interviewer turn or an interruption, and, in the sessions without interviewer turns, at a new participant turn. Segments are built from the kept words only. Their x0 windows stop halfway to dropped words and 20 ms short of interviewer turns, and are stored with the segments, so that the listening check and x0 cut the same audio. The build script reports the dropped share by PHQ-8 class and gender, the drops by cause, the parts per segment by class and the clip lengths.
- Listening check of the rebuilt speech: the seed comes from the cut settings (102, or 104 once the guard is widened to the fallback), segments whose audio shares anything with a clip already heard are left out (`listening_checks` in `configs/pilot/data.yaml`), and the clips are cut from the windows stored with the segments, after checking that the segments were built from the pinned inputs and settings.
- opensmile 2.6.0 in the `pipeline` extra, for the eGeMAPS voice measures of the re-voiced clip check. jiwer moves from the `pipeline` extra to the main dependencies, so that the word-check tests run on the Mac and in CI. No other locked version changes.
- Kokoro-82M and faster-whisper large-v3 pinned to Hugging Face commits in `configs/pilot/models.yaml`, and a GPU job that checks the clip-version tools on a fixed public text before any clip is built: Kokoro loads offline with every tensor of its file in place (`check_weights` loads the file again and compares every weight; the scale and shift weights that Kokoro adds to its AdaIN norms, absent from the file, are accepted only at 1 and 0), its espeak fallback is on (if not, the job logs the real reason), faster-whisper and wav2vec2 run in float16 and read the text back with a WER of at most 0.10, and opensmile gives all 88 eGeMAPS values. The job also writes the text in the seven candidate voices for listening.
- espeak-ng 1.52.0 built from source into the OMNI workspace by `scripts/build_espeak_ng.sh`, which `scripts/setup_omni_env.sh` runs: the same commit and build options as the library bundled with espeakng-loader 0.2.4, but compiled with the system compilers and as position-independent code, because that library needs glibc 2.29 and OMNI has 2.28. Kokoro's text front end uses this build for words outside its lexicon (`listening_beyond_words.espeak`); without it such words would get no sound.
- Glossary rows for the four clip versions, eGeMAPS, ICC, leakage AUROC and the word check.
- The cut of `x0` in three steps that the other clip versions reuse: cut each part's window, fade it, and join the parts with the 300 ms gaps, returning where each part starts and ends. Its output is unchanged, bit for bit. The segment loader, the listening level and the reader of a listening check's key move from `scripts/listening_check.py` into `clips.py`, so that the clip-building scripts share them.
- `runinfo.item_seed`: the seed of one item, such as a part of a re-voiced clip, derived from the run seed and the item's keys with SHA-256, so that it is the same in every process and on every machine.
- Build of the original, muffled and reversed clips (`x0`, `x2`, `rev`) of every segment, part by part, as fixed in the pilot protocol (section "Clip versions"): a CPU job (`scripts/slurm/build_views.sbatch`) first rebuilds `x0` of the 20 clips of the listening check made from the pinned segments and stops unless the listening copy of each one (scaled and written as the listening check wrote it) equals the heard file, sample for sample. It then writes every view as 16 kHz mono 16-bit FLAC (each sample rounded to the nearest 16-bit step; a value outside the range stops the job instead of being clipped), reads each file back, checks that the three views of a segment have the same parts, silent gaps and parts that start and end at 0, and writes a manifest and `run.json` per view. `x2` is low-passed in float64 and set to the RMS of `x0`, with the peak guard; `rev` reverses each part in 250 ms windows before its fades. The backup of check F4 (`reverse_whole`) is coded and tested but built only if `rev` fails the check. `write_json` now writes NumPy numbers, and `runinfo.environment` records package versions, the CPU and the SHA-256 of `uv.lock`.
- Word-check sample (`wordcheck.draw_sample` and `scripts/draw_wordcheck_sample.py`, run on an OMNI login node from a clean checkout): five segments per participant drawn with seed 105 without replacement, or all of a participant's segments if there are fewer, with participants and segments taken in ascending ID so that the draw depends only on the seed. It is written with its SHA-256 for pinning, before any clip is transcribed.
- The engineering conventions describe the repository layout as it is: flat modules in the package, one script per pipeline step in `scripts/`, a job file of the same name for GPU and long CPU steps, and output folders named after what they hold. The session guard is `sessions.py`.
- The clip versions `x0`, `x2` and `rev` of all 5,883 segments (built on 2026-11-22, job 12557091) and the word-check sample of 943 segments, pinned in `configs/pilot/data.yaml` (`view_runs` and `wordcheck_sample`) by the SHA-256 of their manifest and sample files, with the result in the pilot protocol (section "Clip versions").
- `segments.segment_parts`: the words of each part of every segment, named by their transcript line and the indices of the part's first and last word, from the words table of a segments run, which holds no text. It shares its code with `build_segments`, whose output is unchanged, bit for bit, and it refuses a part whose words are not one unbroken run of one line.
- Slurm rules: `submit.sh` submits every job with `--no-requeue`, so that a job that Slurm requeues after a node failure never meets its own half-written output, and `common.sh` turns off core dumps. Array jobs run at most 10 GPU tasks or 20 CPU tasks at once, and the conventions give the time limits of `short` (6 h) and of `gpu` and `medium` (1 day).
- The conventions allow the clips of a listening check to be copied to the Mac, only to be heard, as the earlier listening checks and the planned check of the re-voiced clip need; all other data stays on OMNI.

### Changed

- 2026-11-12: the interviewer filter knows 70 of Ellie's scripted lines instead of 10, in her own wording only, because 1.6% of the segments still held her questions in their words.
- 2026-11-13: an interviewer detector is pre-registered in the pilot protocol, with its settings, gates and one fallback in `configs/pilot/data.yaml`, because the listening check heard the interviewer in 4 of 20 clips that the speaker-verification filter had kept.
- 2026-11-13: the pilot runs twice, because the interviewer detector failed its premise gate: a preliminary run on the current speech, then the main run once the interviewer's times from the DAIC-WOZ transcripts allow her to be cut out. Settings fixed in the first run carry over.
- 2026-11-18: the pilot runs once, on speech with the interviewer cut out by her times in the official DAIC-WOZ transcripts, because the transcripts arrived before any training. The preliminary run and the Whisper-based interviewer finder are dropped. Session 440's transcript comes from a public copy checked against the official files, because its official archive is cut off on the USC server.
- 2026-11-18: the cut rule is fixed in the pilot protocol, with its settings in `configs/pilot/data.yaml` (`daicwoz_cut`), because the decision of the same day left the exact rule to be fixed before the speech is rebuilt. A word is kept only inside the participant's turns, at least 200 ms from the interviewer's turns (400 ms in the four sessions with offsets) and within the interview. The offsets of sessions 318, 321, 341 and 362 come from the timing check, and the interruptions in 373 and 444 are widened by 10 s on each side and treated like interviewer turns. If the new listening check fails, the guard is widened once to 500 ms and checked again on new segments. On the word times of 2026-11-12 the rule drops 4.77% of the aligned words, with no significant difference between the PHQ-8 classes.
- 2026-11-18: the line selection with 70 of the interviewer's scripted lines is pinned in `configs/pilot/data.yaml`, so that the word times and the segments are rebuilt from it; the earlier pin still named the selection with 10 lines, from which the word times and segments of 2026-11-12 were built.
- 2026-11-18: in sessions 451, 458 and 480, which have no interviewer turns, the margins of the x0 cut also stop at the edge of the participant's turn, because the first build of the speech kept 10.5 s of margin audio in the gaps between participant turns there, where the interviewer's untranscribed voice may be. Decided before the listening check.
- 2026-11-18: how segments fill the bags is fixed in the pilot protocol ("Segments and bags") and in `configs/pilot/data.yaml` (`bags`), before training, because 39 participants have fewer than 16 segments and one has only 3. In each epoch a participant gives as many whole bags of 4 as fit in at most 16 of their segments; leftover segments wait for a later epoch, no segment is repeated, and a participant with fewer than 4 segments gives no training bag but is still scored.
- 2026-11-20: the part-by-part build of every clip version, the settings of `x2` and `rev`, the levels, the files and the word-check sample are fixed in the pilot protocol (section "Clip versions") and in `configs/pilot/data.yaml` (`views` and `word_check`), before any clip version is built, because the study design did not say whether a filter or a window runs over the whole clip, which would spill sound into the 300 ms gaps, and it said "turn" where a part is meant, as check F4 did for its backup. Every view is built part by part: `x1` makes one Kokoro call per part; `x2` filters each part of `x0` on its own (an 8th-order Butterworth low-pass with its −3 dB point at 400 Hz, run forwards and backwards) and then gets the RMS of `x0`; `rev` plays each part's audio backwards in 250 ms windows from the part's first sample, before the 10 ms fades of `x0`. The backup of check F4 plays each whole part backwards. `x0` keeps the recording's level, and an `x1` or `x2` clip whose peak is above 0.99 once its RMS is set is scaled down to a peak of 0.99 and counted. Every view is stored as 16-bit FLAC with a manifest. The word check (check F4 and the checks of `x1`) transcribes five segments per participant, drawn with seed 105. The settings that only `x1` has and the rules of the word check follow before `x1` is built and before any clip is transcribed. The bullet "Cut" of the section "Speech used" now says "part" where it said "turn" for the 0.5 s between two parts; the meaning is unchanged.
- 2026-11-22: after the filter, each part of `x2` gets the 10 ms fades of `x0` again (pilot protocol, section "Clip versions", bullet "Muffled clip"), before any clip version is built, because the forwards-backwards filter spreads each part's edges by a few milliseconds. Without the second fade the muffled parts would no longer start and end at exactly 0, and each join with a silent gap would get a small step that can click, which the fades of `x0` exist to prevent. On synthetic audio the step was small where a part ends in a quiet margin and largest where speech runs into the fade.
- 2026-11-22: the settings of the re-voiced clip `x1` are fixed in the pilot protocol (section "Clip versions", bullet "Re-voiced clip") and in `configs/pilot/data.yaml` (`views.x1`), before `x1` is built: the voices, the text that Kokoro reads, the seeds, and how each part is resampled, trimmed, faded and levelled. Two of them depart from the earlier plan. The voices are assigned within each gender by PHQ-8 class and then ID, alternating between the two voices, instead of by participant ID alone, because an assignment by ID can split one gender's positive participants unevenly between its voices by chance, and B1 and CPG train on `x1`, so a model could learn the label from the voice. Each part is resampled to 16 kHz before it is trimmed and faded, and the trim keeps 10 ms beyond the first and last sound, because on synthetic audio a fade made before resampling left the part edges off 0, which the build's own check refuses, and a trim without that margin put the fades on the first and last sounds of a part.
